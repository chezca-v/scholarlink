How It Works

Your Scholarship Journey,
Step by Step

From profile setup to receiving your scholarship — ScholarLink makes the entire process simple, transparent, and guided for every type of user.

Student Applicant
Organization
Evaluator
Step 01

Create Your Profile

Sign up and complete a 4-step setup wizard. Input your academic background — GWA, course, year level — along with your financial status, region, and household information. Upload key documents to your secure Student Wallet to reuse across all applications.

4-step guided profile wizard
Secure Document Wallet for one-time uploads
Philippine grading scale (1.00–5.00) supported
01
Step 02

Discover Matched Scholarships

Our Gemini AI matching engine analyzes your profile and surfaces the most relevant opportunities from government, private, institutional, and international scholarship providers — ranked by your compatibility score. No more manual searching.

AI match score (0–100%) per scholarship
Eligibility-gated browsing — no unqualified listings
20+ scholarships including CHED, DOST, and private orgs
02
Step 03

Apply with One Click

Use the 3-step Apply Wizard to submit your application. ScholarLink auto-fills information from your profile, and documents from your Student Wallet are attached automatically — so you never re-upload the same file twice.

Profile auto-fill — no repeated data entry
Document reuse from your Wallet
Reference code generated (e.g. GD-2026-00001)
03
Step 04

Track Your Application

Follow your application through a real-time visual timeline. From Submitted → Document Review → Under Evaluation → Final Decision. Receive SMS notifications (via ESP32 + SIM800L hardware) and email alerts at every stage change.

Color-coded live status timeline
Email notifications at every stage
Deadline reminders at 14, 7, 3, and 1 day
04
Step 05

Receive Your Decision

Get notified instantly when a decision is made. If approved, next steps are outlined clearly. If rejected, detailed feedback including the rejection reason and suggested alternative scholarships are provided — so a "no" is never the end of the road.

Instant notification on decision
Structured rejection feedback with reason codes
Alternative scholarship suggestions on rejection
05
Step 06

Join the Scholar Community

Connect with other scholars, access mentorship networks, and stay updated on new scholarship opportunities. Your ScholarLink profile grows with your academic journey — one profile, every scholarship, for life.

Scholar network and mentorship connections
New scholarship alerts as they go live
Google Calendar export for deadlines

Student Wallet

Upload documents once. Attach to any application instantly. ScholarLink securely stores your transcripts, certificates, and IDs — with expiry date tracking and revision requests.

Real-Time Notifications

SMS via hardware (ESP32 + SIM800L) and email notifications ensure you never miss a deadline or status update. Reminders fire at 14, 7, 3, and 1-day intervals automatically.

Deadline Calendar Export

Export scholarship deadlines directly to Google Calendar. Important dates stay visible in your schedule without any manual entry.

AI Chatbot Assistant

An always-available chatbot guides students through eligibility questions, application requirements, and platform navigation — powered by Gemini AI.

Blind Screening

Evaluators review anonymized applications — applicant names, addresses, gender, and schools are hidden — ensuring merit-based, bias-free selection for every scholarship.
