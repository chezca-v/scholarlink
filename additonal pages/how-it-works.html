How It Works

Your Scholarship Journey, Step by Step

From profile setup to receiving your scholarship — ScholarLink makes the entire process simple, transparent, and guided.

The Process

Three Paths, One Platform

ScholarLink supports students, scholarship organizations, and evaluators — each with a tailored experience designed to reduce friction and increase transparency at every stage.

1
Create Your Profile

Sign up and complete the 4-step setup wizard. Input your academic background, financial status, region, and upload key documents to your secure Document Wallet.

2
Discover Matched Scholarships

Our AI matching engine analyzes your profile and surfaces the most relevant opportunities from government, private, institutional, and international scholarship providers.

3
Apply with One Click

Use the 3-step Apply Wizard to submit your application. ScholarLink auto-fills information from your profile, and documents from your wallet are attached automatically.

4
Track Your Application

Follow your application through a real-time visual timeline. Receive SMS and email notifications at every stage — submission, review, evaluation, and decision.

5
Receive Your Decision

Get notified instantly when a decision is made. If approved, next steps are outlined clearly. If rejected, detailed feedback and suggested alternatives are provided.

6
Join the Scholar Community

Connect with other scholars, access mentorship networks, and stay updated on new opportunities. Your ScholarLink profile grows with your academic journey.

Platform Features

Everything You Need, Built In

AI-Powered Matching Engine

ScholarLink uses Gemini AI to analyze your full profile — GWA, course, region, financial need, extracurriculars — and ranks scholarship opportunities by compatibility score. No more manual searching through dozens of listings.

Document Wallet

Upload documents once. Attach to any application instantly. ScholarLink securely stores your transcripts, certificates, and IDs.

Real-Time Notifications

SMS via hardware (ESP32 + SIM800L) and email notifications ensure you never miss a deadline or status update.

Deadline Calendar Export

Export scholarship deadlines directly to Google Calendar so important dates are always visible in your schedule.

AI Chatbot Assistant

An always-available chatbot guides students through eligibility questions, application requirements, and platform navigation.

Blind Screening

Evaluators review anonymized applications to reduce bias and ensure merit-based, equitable selection across all applicants.
