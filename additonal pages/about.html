About ScholarLink

Bridging Filipino Students to Scholarship Opportunities

A platform built for every aspiring scholar — from Luzon to Mindanao — making access to education funding simple, transparent, and equitable.

Our Mission

Empowering the Next Generation of Filipino Leaders

ScholarLink was built to solve a persistent problem: deserving Filipino students miss out on scholarships simply because they didn't know about them or couldn't navigate complex application processes.


We consolidate government, private, and institutional scholarships into one trusted platform — with clear eligibility guides, real-time status tracking, and a support system that speaks your language.

Our Story

From a Capstone to a Movement

ScholarLink started as a university capstone project after our founders noticed how many of their classmates were unaware of scholarships they were fully eligible for.

What began as a simple aggregator grew into a full platform when CHED and DOST partners recognized the need for a centralized, student-friendly scholarship portal — one that could serve SHS graduates, college freshmen, and working scholars alike.

Today, ScholarLink serves students across all 17 regions of the Philippines, partnering with government agencies, state universities, and private foundations to keep educational opportunity within reach.
