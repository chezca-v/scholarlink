About ScholarLink

Bridging Filipino Students to Scholarship Opportunities

A platform built for every aspiring scholar — from Luzon to Mindanao — making access to education funding simple, transparent, and equitable.

Our Mission

Empowering the Next Generation of Filipino Leaders

ScholarLink was developed to address a critical gap in access to educational funding. Many deserving Filipino students are unable to pursue scholarships due to limited awareness, fragmented information, and complex application processes.


Our mission is to simplify and centralize scholarship access by providing a platform that consolidates opportunities from government agencies, private institutions, and academic organizations.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Transparency

Every scholarship listing includes complete requirements, timelines, and decision criteria — no hidden steps, no surprises.

Accessibility

Designed for students across all regions and backgrounds, with multilingual support and mobile-first experience.

Integrity

We verify every scholarship and partner institution. We do not charge students any fees — ever.

Community

We connect scholars with mentors, alumni, and peers — building networks that last beyond the scholarship period.

Innovation

We continuously improve our platform with smart matching, AI-assisted guidance, and real-time tracking tools.

Equity

We actively surface scholarships for underserved groups — PWDs, indigenous peoples, and students from conflict-affected areas.

Our Story

From a Capstone to a Movement

ScholarLink began as an academic capstone project with the objective of addressing the challenges students face when searching for scholarships. The founders identified a recurring issue: many qualified students were missing opportunities simply due to lack of access to reliable information.

What started as a basic scholarship listing system evolved into a comprehensive platform that supports the entire application process. As the project progressed, it expanded its scope to include multiple scholarship providers and improved user experience features.

Today, ScholarLink continues to grow as a platform dedicated to helping Filipino students navigate educational opportunities more effectively, with the long-term goal of contributing to a more equitable education system.
