Why Partner With Us

A Smarter Way to Administer Scholarships

Managing scholarship applications manually is time-consuming and prone to error. ScholarLink gives your organization a centralized, digital-first platform to post opportunities, define eligibility criteria, and oversee the entire screening lifecycle — all in one place.


From government agencies and private foundations to universities and corporations, ScholarLink supports every type of scholarship provider in reaching the right applicants faster and with greater fairness.

Getting started

How it works for you

1
Register your organization

2
Post your scholarship

3
Review matched applicants

4

What You Get

Platform Benefits

Wider Reach

Instantly connect with thousands of student applicants across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao through a single platform.

Blind Screening

Our system hides applicant names, addresses, and gender during evaluation — ensuring bias-free, merit-based selection.

Smart Matching

The intelligent eligibility engine filters out unqualified applicants before they reach your review queue — saving time at every stage.

Analytics Dashboard

Access real-time reports on application volume, document completeness rates, and fund distribution metrics.

Document Verification

Uploaded student documents (ITR, IDs, certificates) are systematically validated for presence and format before reaching reviewers.

Deadline Automation

Automated reminders notify applicants at 14, 7, 3, and 1-day intervals — reducing last-minute rushes and incomplete submissions.

Who Can Join

Open to All Scholarship Providers

ScholarLink is designed to accommodate every type of organization that offers educational funding to Filipino students. Whether you're a government body, a private corporation, or an academic institution, our platform scales to your needs.

Each organization account is verified and managed by the ScholarLink superadmin, ensuring platform integrity. Once onboarded, your administrators have full control over scholarship postings, evaluator assignments, and applicant communications.

There are no platform fees for scholarship providers — our mission is to maximize access for students, not to create barriers for the institutions that support them.

Admin Capabilities

Everything You Need to Run Your Program

Scholarship CRUD

Create, update, and manage scholarship postings with full control over eligibility criteria, scoring weights, deadlines, and "Coming Soon" indicators with countdown timers.

Evaluator Management

Assign dedicated evaluators to specific scholarships, manage review queues, and receive instant alerts when evaluations are completed.

Dynamic Scoring Engine

Customize the weighting between merit-based (GPA) and needs-based (income) criteria per scholarship, ensuring your program's priorities are reflected precisely.

Document Review

Systematically verify, approve, reject, or request revisions on applicant-submitted files (ITRs, valid IDs, certificates) at every stage of screening.

Application Analytics

View real-time dashboards with application volume by stage, document completion rates, evaluator throughput, and fund distribution insights.

Feedback & Alternatives

When rejecting an applicant, provide mandatory feedback and attach alternative scholarship suggestions — maintaining a positive experience even for unsuccessful applicants.
