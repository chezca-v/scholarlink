Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ScholarLink free for students?
Yes — ScholarLink is completely free for all student applicants. We do not charge any fees for browsing scholarships, creating a profile, uploading documents, or submitting applications. Our mission is to remove barriers to educational funding, not add new ones.
How does the matching engine work?
Our intelligent matching engine compares your academic profile — GPA, course, income bracket, and location — against each scholarship's eligibility criteria. It calculates a match percentage and prevents you from submitting to scholarships you're not qualified for, saving you time and avoiding rejection.
What is Blind Screening?
Blind Screening is a fairness feature built into the evaluator portal. When enabled, it automatically hides identifying information — including your name, home address, gender, and optionally your school — so that evaluators score applications based purely on academic merit and financial need, not personal background.
How do I register my organization?
Organization accounts are created and verified by the ScholarLink superadmin. To get started, use the contact form on this page and select "Organization Partnership" as the subject. Our team will reach out to guide you through the onboarding process and set up your admin account.
What documents do I need to upload?
Common documents stored in your Student Wallet include: government-issued IDs, Income Tax Returns (ITR) or Certificate of Tax Exemption, academic transcripts, certificates of enrollment, and certificates of good moral character. Specific requirements vary by scholarship — each listing will clearly state what's needed.
Can I track my application status?
Yes. Your Application Tracker shows a real-time, color-coded timeline of every active application — from "Submitted" through "Document Review," "Under Evaluation," "Interview Scheduled," and "Final Decision." You'll also receive in-app, email, and SMS notifications at each stage change.
